02

The opportunity

Retailers have approached us about stocking Tom Brown. We have had to turn them down.

No Nigerian supermarket can legally stock a packaged food product without NAFDAC registration. NAFDAC will not register a home kitchen — registration requires compliant premises and equipment. So we sell the only way currently open to us: jar by jar, direct to customers, by dispatch rider.

That ceiling is the whole opportunity. Demand is proven and the product is finished. What changes with capital is not whether people want Tom Brown — it is how many of them we can legally reach. A NAFDAC certificate converts a dispatch-rider business into one that can sit on a supermarket shelf and sell while we sleep.

+ + + + We are not asking you to fund a search for customers. We are asking you to fund the licence and the capacity to serve the customers who have already asked for us.

05

Your returns

You are paid a share of what we actually sell. There is no fixed instalment, which means there is nothing we can default on — and nothing we would ever need to fund from a later investor's money.

When payments start, and at what rate

Payments begin 60 days after the money reaches us — not when NAFDAC issues. You start receiving money from month three, out of current trading, while the facility is still being fitted out. Early amounts are small because they are a share of today's takings, but they are real and they are monthly.

The rate runs at half until six months after our first NAFDAC-approved sale, then steps up to the full 20%. That window is anchored to the licence rather than the calendar, because the tightest months are the ones straight after registration — new rent, new staff and bulk stock, with retail revenue still climbing. The repayment cap is unchanged; the ramp defers part of your return rather than reducing it.

What a month looks like

At the full rate, ₦4,000,000 of company gross revenue pays the pool ₦800,000. Split across a ₦7.5M / ₦5M / ₦2.5M round, that is ₦400,000, ₦266,000 and ₦134,000 respectively. While ramping it is half those figures.

On a weak month of ₦1,500,000, the pool receives ₦300,000 at the full rate. Your payment falls with our sales and rises with them — which is the point.

07

Questions you may have

What happens to my money if NAFDAC refuses registration?

By that point the capital will have been spent on premises, equipment, filing and stock. Equipment and materials retain resale value, but you should assume a substantial loss. This is the largest single risk in the investment, and it is why the 18-month long-stop exists: rather than let a small revenue charge run against this business for decades, we restructure, with conversion of the outstanding balance to equity as the default remedy.

How do I verify the revenue you pay me on?

Your payment is calculated on gross revenue, which is materially harder to manipulate than profit. You receive a monthly statement with supporting bank statements, and the agreement grants you the right to inspect our books or appoint an independent accountant at your own cost. Before investing, our management accounts and bank statements are open to you.

Why a revenue share rather than a fixed monthly return?

Because a fixed return we could not always meet would eventually have to be paid out of somebody else's capital, and that is how these arrangements collapse. A share of sales adjusts on its own — a slow month is a smaller payment rather than a missed one. It is a lower ceiling in exchange for a structure that holds under pressure. We think that trade is the right one, and we would rather offer you something we can honour.

When exactly do payments start?

60 days after the funds reach us. Not when NAFDAC issues, not when the first retail order lands — 60 days. You will be receiving money from month three, out of current trading, while the facility is still being fitted out. The amounts are small at that stage, but we would rather you saw the arrangement working from the beginning than waited a year on a promise.

Why is the rate halved during the ramp?

The tightest months are the ones straight after registration: new rent, new staff and bulk stock, with retail revenue still climbing. Taking the full rate through that window would starve the operation exactly when it needs to establish itself on shelf — which would hurt your return as much as ours. The half rate is tied to the licence rather than the calendar for that reason, and your cap is unchanged.

What if NAFDAC drags on for years?

An 18-month long-stop applies. If the certificate has not been issued by then, both parties meet to restructure, with conversion of the outstanding balance into additional equity as the default remedy at a formula fixed at signing. We put this in deliberately: without it, a failed registration would leave a small revenue charge running against this business for decades, which serves neither of us.

What say do I have in the business?

None in daily operations — that remains with the founder, and we think you would rather we ran the business than managed a committee. You do receive monthly reporting, and your consent is required before we take on debt above an agreed threshold or issue new shares.

Can I invest in Tom Brown only, and not the cafe?

Yes — we can reduce the capital and restructure the deal around the food line alone. Note that both lines will run from the funded facility and share overheads, so the accounting has to be drawn carefully, and the numbers on this page would change. Come and talk to us about it.

Can I exit before the cap is reached?

There is no redemption right and no market for the shares. The revenue share runs until the cap is met. Any share transfer is subject to our articles and would require a willing buyer.

Why is the raise no longer staged?

We had structured this in two tranches, with the second released once the certificate was issued. On working through real costs, ₦10,000,000 is what clearing NAFDAC actually takes — premises, equipment, filing and utilities. Holding back the balance would have left us with a licence and no capacity to supply at volume, which is a worse business than it looks and a worse outcome for you. We replaced the tranche protection with milestone reporting and the long-stop.

What is the minimum, and can I do a different amount?

₦1,000,000 is the minimum, and the per-million rule scales cleanly to any figure above it. Amounts or structures outside the standard terms can be discussed.

What can I see before committing?

Certificate of incorporation and CAC status report, tax identification, management accounts and bank statements, quotations for premises, equipment and NAFDAC consultancy, and the draft investment agreement. We will send the pack on request and expect you to have your own lawyer review it.
